// Event

Reopening the mountain for something new.

We’re reactivating underused lift and hut infrastructure, turning the mountain into our stage. Super Verticale is much more than a trail run. It’s a gathering for those who live for uncompromising mountain sports, good food and music vibes, and the culture of nature.

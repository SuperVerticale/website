// RACE MODES

ONE RACE.
MULTIPLE WAYS
TO COMPETE.

Fastest overall wins. From the start at the bottom to the finish at the top, every second counts. The Sprinter is the one who conquers the full 10.1 km fastest.

Own the steepest section. One defined section of the course puts pure uphill power to the test. The fastest time on this section takes the Climber title.

Consistency beats speed. Split times are recorded throughout the race, and the smallest difference between your fastest and slowest split wins. It’s not about one perfect section — it’s about keeping it together from bottom to top.

Save something for the end. The final defined section is its own race. Whoever delivers the fastest finish takes the title — because sometimes the race only really starts when the legs are already burning.

Some things are better done together. Bring your crew and race as one. The Herd rewards the strongest team performance rather than a single individual result.

The crowd gets a say. Every runner can become a contender, regardless of their finishing time. Use the race bib to vote for the person who impressed, inspired or entertained you most.

Capture the day. Submit your best Super Verticale moment and let the community decide which image tells the story of the day. It doesn’t have to be the fastest runner — it just has to be the shot people remember.

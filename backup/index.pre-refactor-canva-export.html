// Event

Ending the summer off-season in the Alps: We’re reactivating underused lift and hut infrastructure, turning the mountain into our stage. Super Verticale is much more than a trail run. It’s a gathering for those who live for uncompromising mountain sports, good food vibes, and the culture of nature.

Get ready to sweat, climb, and celebrate your way up the mountain at the first and limited SUPER VERTICALE. Starting from the Petzenbahn Talstation, this intense 10 km trail run tackles 1,000 m of elevation gain straight up to a breathtaking viewpoint over Kärnten. Whether you're chasing a personal best, hunting a segment win, or simply out for the view, expect a pure vertical work. At the summit, handpicked brands, drinks, and good food await, because the race gets you there, but the people will let you stay.
